# Privacy Policy — Gaffer

**Effective Date:** March 20, 2026
**Last Updated:** October 13, 2026

Gaffer ("the App") is a soccer analytics application. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Data
- **Google OAuth:** When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture. We do not receive or store your Google password.

### Profile Data
- Display name, avatar, favorite teams, and favorite leagues — provided during onboarding.

### Content You Create
- **Watch Parties:** messages you post in a watch party, visible to its members.
- **Feedback:** what you write in Send Feedback, plus any screenshots you attach and recent app logs, used to investigate the issue you report.

### Usage Data
- App opens, screens viewed, matches tapped, watch parties created/joined — collected via Firebase Analytics to improve the app experience.

### Crash Data
- Firebase Crashlytics collects anonymous crash reports (device model, OS version, stack traces) to help us fix bugs.

### Push Notification Tokens
- If you enable push notifications, we store your device token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) to deliver watch party alerts. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- **Authentication:** To create and secure your account.
- **Personalization:** To show scores, fixtures, and standings for your favorite teams and leagues.
- **Watch Parties:** To enable private match chat rooms with other users.
- **Analytics:** To understand usage patterns and improve the app.
- **Crash Reporting:** To identify and fix bugs.
- **Feedback:** To investigate and fix the issues you report.
- **Notifications:** To alert you about watch party activity.

---

## 3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on [Supabase](https://supabase.com) (PostgreSQL) infrastructure hosted on AWS. Firebase services (Crashlytics, Analytics, Cloud Messaging) are hosted by Google. All network communication uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

- **We do not sell your personal data to third parties.**
- We share data only with the service providers listed above (Supabase, Firebase/Google) as necessary to operate the App, and with **GitHub**, where Send Feedback reports (text, screenshots, logs) are filed in our private issue tracker.
- We may disclose information if required by law.

### YouTube Videos
The App shows match highlight videos using YouTube API Services. Videos play in YouTube's embedded player, which is operated by Google; when you play a video, Google may collect information (such as cookies and device data) under its own policies. By watching videos in the App you agree to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), and Google's handling of that data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not send your personal data to YouTube. To decide which videos can play for you, the App reads your phone's region setting on your device; it is not sent to us or stored.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

- Your account data is retained as long as your account is active.
- Signing out ends your session on that device; it does not delete your data.
- To delete your account and its data, email us at the address below. We will delete it, including your profile and preferences.
- Crash and analytics data is retained per Google Firebase's default retention policies (typically 90 days for Crashlytics, 14 months for Analytics).

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Gaffer is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

---

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by the "Effective Date" at the top. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
